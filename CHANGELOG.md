## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-02

### Added

- shadcn registry with 21 components: `pill-button`, `chip`, `field`, `event-card`, `polaroid`, `section-heading`, `split-words`, `reveal`, `sticker`, `marquee`, `pluck-strings`, `brew-cup`, `magnetic`, `tilt-card`, `hero-scene`, `toy-train`, `train-stepper`, `cursor`, `throw-box`, `reorder-list`, `swipe-deck`.
- `kettle-theme` with ten colour tokens, hard shadows, easings and keyframes, plus `kettle-all` to install everything.
- Helpers pulled in automatically: `use-on-screen`, `use-fine-pointer`, `tf`, `leaf-pick`.
- Custom cursor with six variants and `data-cursor` labels.
- Docs site with the cursor lab, motion gallery, floating dock header, scroll-spy sidebar and train preloader.
- MIT license, contributing guide, issue templates and CI.
